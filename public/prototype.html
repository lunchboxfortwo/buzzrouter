The smallest complete BuzzRouter

A public directory that answers one question well: which Buzz community is worth joining? The MVP validates discovery and trust before attempting workflow routing.

Recommended cut
Ship in MVP The complete discovery loop
  • Browse, search, and category filters
  • Community profiles with native relay joins
  • Trending, Top rated, New, and Most joined views
  • One rating per verified identity
  • Community submission and relay verification
  • Public ranking methodology
Measure immediately Signals that prove the wedge
  • Directory visitor to relay join conversion
  • Searches that end with a profile view
  • Ratings per joined community
  • Weekly returning explorers
  • Operator-submitted listings verified
Defer Until discovery has pull
  • DNS, handles, or a competing naming layer
  • Workflow execution and agent orchestration
  • Payments and community monetization
  • Cross-community dependency graphs
  • Fully decentralized indexing protocol
Milestone Product behavior Exit test Main risk
1. Seed Curated directory, profiles, relay joins, and operator submissions. 50 credible listings across at least five categories. An empty directory cannot demonstrate discovery value.
2. Trust Verified ratings, confidence thresholds, activity signals, and public ranking. At least 30% of joins produce a later rating. Low rating volume makes rankings feel arbitrary.
3. Pull Community claims, moderation tools, saved communities, and weekly updates. 25% weekly returning explorers and organic operator submissions. Operators optimize listings without improving communities.
